How it works

Claude reads every word, so you don't have to rewatch the hour.

1

Drop in your video

azclips listens and writes down every word with its exact time. This happens on your own computer.

2

Claude picks the moments

Claude looks for strong hooks, stories, punchlines and answers that make sense on their own, and scores each one.

3

Get clips ready to post

Each clip is cut to 9:16 with bold captions burned in, plus a title and a caption with hashtags you can copy.

Install

Unzip, double-click, start clipping.

  1. Download azclips-Windows.zip and unzip it, for example into Documents.
  2. Open the folder and double-click azclips.exe.
  3. A small black window opens, then azclips opens in your browser. Keep the black window open while you work. Close it to quit.
  4. Your first video takes a bit longer while azclips downloads its speech model (about 150 MB).

Questions

Good to know

Does it work on Mac or my phone?

Not yet. azclips runs on Windows 10 and 11 for now.

What kind of videos work best?

Videos where people talk: podcasts, interviews, streams, talks, lessons and vlogs. Claude picks clips by reading what's said, so videos with little speech, like gaming without commentary or music, give it much less to work with.

Is it really free?

Yes. The app is free, and the free mode uses a free claude.ai account. Automatic mode uses your own Anthropic API key, which costs a few cents per video, paid to Anthropic.

How long does it take?

Most of the time goes into turning speech into text on your computer. On a typical laptop, a one-hour video takes roughly 10 to 40 minutes, then a few seconds per clip.

Which languages does it support?

It detects the language automatically and works with English, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and many more. Titles and captions come back in the video's own language.

Can I clip other people's videos?

Only upload videos you made or have permission to use. azclips is built for creators clipping their own content.
